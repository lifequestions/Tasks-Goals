Nothing added yet — and a page works perfectly without any

When an answer needs something to look at, it can be attached to that question rather than dumped in an album:

  • A map of the street or town he was born in.
  • A photograph of the house, his first car, his first child.
  • Papers — a certificate, a ticket, a letter, a payslip.

Each one sits under the answer it belongs to, captioned in his words.

    Recording this one

    1. Phone landscape, propped on something, at his eye level.
    2. Sit off to the side of the camera and ask him — he'll talk to you, not the lens.
    3. Ask the question, then stay quiet. The good part usually comes after the first pause.
    4. Work through the prompts above if he dries up. Aim for four to six minutes.
    5. Upload to YouTube as unlisted, then paste the eleven-character video ID into this question.
