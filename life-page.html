Her life, in her own voice

Margaret Ellen Doyle

Born 17 March 1938, Ballina, Co. Mayo — Died 8 November 2024, Coventry

She came over at seventeen with two cardigans in a case, nursed nights in Coventry for forty years, married Frank in 1961 and raised Anne, Tom and Aoife. In 2022 her children sat her down with a phone and started asking questions.
