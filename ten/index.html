Luis James de Souza · born 4 February 1943, Bangalore

Ten ways to build his page

Same man, same twelve questions, same five recordings — ten different shapes. Open any of them, press any still to play. Everything is live, so you can judge them the way the family would.

Easy answers first

Name, parents, schools, first job. Typed in once, no camera. It means the page looks like something on day one.

One main film

Every layout leads with a single film, so a visitor never has to choose where to start.

Stills, not players

Each answer shows a still pulled from YouTube. The player only loads when somebody presses it — which is why hosting stays free.

Shaped like a video channel — dark or light, whichever your machine is set to

Latest — the list of answers, with four different tops. All four carry the birthplace map

The original ten

The four at the top all share 05’s list of answers and all end with the map of Bangalore and the two schools. They differ only in the top: 13 and 14 lead with a photograph, 11 with the film, 12 with the portrait and record card. Say which and I’ll build the real one properly.

Five of the twelve answers currently play borrowed interview clips, so the mechanism can be seen working. They are replaced by pasting in his own video ID.
