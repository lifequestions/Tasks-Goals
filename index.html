Host one in your city

It takes a room and six people. We give you the questions, the running order and whatever else we have learned from doing it; you pick the date.

  1. Tell us where you are. One email. We’ll say what has worked in a city that size.
  2. Pick a room and a date. A back room, a front room, a table in a quiet pub. Six people is enough.
  3. We send you the questions and how to run the evening, so you aren’t making it up on the night.
  4. Your city goes on this page so people nearby can find it.

There’s nothing to pay and nothing to sign. We’d rather there were evenings running in twenty cities than a business in one.
